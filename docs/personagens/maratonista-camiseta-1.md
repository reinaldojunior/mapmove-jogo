# Personagem: maratonista nº 1 (fichas para gerar em 3D)

Três vistas do mesmo personagem para o Meshy (Image to 3D, multi-view). Pose A com espaço entre braços e corpo
e entre as pernas, para o esqueleto não grudar o braço no quadril.

Depois de gerar: Remesh ~10.000 polígonos, textura 1024, rigging humanoide (~1,75 m) e exportar um GLB para
cada animação (corrida, parado, comemoração).

## 1. Vista frontal (imagem principal)

Full body front view concept art of a slender male marathon runner, realistic athletic anatomy, standing in a clean symmetrical A-pose. Arms held away from the body at a 45-degree angle with clear visible empty space between arms and torso/legs, hands relaxed and fingers distinct not touching hips. Legs straight and feet shoulder-width apart with gap between thighs. Wearing a white athletic running singlet with horizontal green and red chest stripes and race bib number 1, black compression shorts, black arm sleeves, and white running shoes. Solid neutral clean light gray background, studio lighting, sharp focus, full body completely in frame from head to toe, character concept sheet style.

## 2. Vista lateral direita

Full body right side profile view of the same slender male marathon runner, exact same character and outfit. Standing upright in an A-pose, right arm angled slightly away from the torso with clear empty gap, fingers not touching the thigh. Legs straight and feet firmly planted. Wearing white running singlet with green and red chest stripes and race bib number 1, black compression shorts, black arm sleeves, and white running shoes. Solid neutral clean light gray background, matching lighting and height, full body completely in frame from head to toe, 90 degree exact side view.

## 3. Vista traseira

Full body back view of the same slender male marathon runner, seen directly from behind, exact same character and outfit. Standing in symmetrical A-pose with arms held at a 45-degree angle away from the body, clear visible empty space between arms and torso/legs, feet shoulder-width apart. Showing racerback white singlet with horizontal green and red stripes and race bib number 1 on the back, black compression shorts, black arm sleeves, and white running shoes. Solid neutral clean light gray background, matching lighting, full body completely in frame from head to toe.
